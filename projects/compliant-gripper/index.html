Sensorized Compliant Gripper

0 Wto hold a grasp · 1.35 € · 2 printed parts

Challenge

Industrial grippers are rigid and spend energy holding fragile objects. Earlier soft grippers in the lab used springs, which released violently.

Solution

Designed and 3D-printed a soft gripper with a passive worm-screw self-locking mechanism. Fixed finger buckling with SimScale FEA and integrated embroidered e-textile sensors for tactile feedback.

Impact

Holds objects at zero power, with 100% detection success on fragile objects, for 1.35 € and 2 printed parts. The flexible design was 2.54× more energy-efficient than the reference. Validated with OptiTrack and integrated on a robot arm.
