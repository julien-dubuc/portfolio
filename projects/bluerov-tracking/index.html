AI-Powered 3D Tracking for BlueROV

4.7 cmprecision at 3 m

Challenge

Track the 3D trajectory of an underwater robot in a test basin without an expensive industrial motion-capture system.

Solution

Python pipeline in a team of 4: YOLOv8 detection trained for the aquatic environment, multi-camera calibration with OpenCV and ArUco, and least-squares stereo triangulation.

Impact

1.2 cm precision at 1.5 m and 4.7 cm at 3 m, validated against Qualisys. Released as open source and praised by the COSMER lab jury.
